## Design rules

- **Colorblind-safe** palette; never encode by color alone — pair with pattern or direct label
- **CIs on every proportion.** A bare bar chart of percentages will draw reviewer fire
- Vector (PDF) for all figures; no rasterized text
- Consistent V-class colors across F4, F5, F6, F7
- Figures readable in **grayscale print** — venue reviewers still print
- Caption states the claim, not the contents. *"Response validation provides no protection against an adaptive adversary"* — not *"ASR by defense condition"*

scripts/build_figures.sh     # regenerates every figure from experiments/results/
