# EffectSeal submission roadmap

Current framing: **Faithful Responses, Faithless Effects: Verified-Effect
Admission for Tool-Using LLM Agents on Untrusted MCP Servers**.
The system combines confined local-state admission and pre-send network
admission with pin-time contracts. Known slack and coverage failures delimit
its result; it does not provide universal safety or semantic intent checking.
Historical roadmap: `paper/history/roadmap-before-20261009-repair.md`.

Target: USENIX Security 2027, Cycle 2. Official CFP checked 2026-10-09:
registration January 19, paper January 26, artifact January 29 (AoE).

limit: 20 total pages. Open Science is required; Ethics is encouraged and included.

Meet the page limit by editing content, not spacing or font changes.

### P1 — scientific limits retained in the paper

- Local held-out false blocks: 6/48 (12.5%); the 5% utility criterion failed.
- Original adaptive admissions: 36/648, including held-out travel.
- Additional cohort adaptive admissions: 12/336 on conekta.
- Stable poisoning passes repeat-session agreement; an independent semantic specification remains necessary.
- Coverage is conditional; the additional cohort includes telemetry/empty payload and correlated packages.
- Actual timing is local HTTP, not production TLS/WAN performance.
- Historical model-agent and baseline results are separately dated, not freshly rescored claims.

These are documented negative results, not unfinished correctness repairs.
Any future attempt to meet a stricter deployment gate needs a new frozen plan
and independent scored cohort. Do not tune on these held-out failures and keep
calling the same data held-out.
